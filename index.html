Who We Are

Built on land we own.
Managed by us. Long term.

Sharma Group was founded in 1975 by Dr. Anurag Sharma. Over five decades, the group has developed more than 2 million square feet of retail, office, logistics, and infrastructure projects across Haryana and Rajasthan — serving FMCG, Automobile, 3PL, Manufacturing, Office, and Retail clients.

Our model is simple: we acquire land, build modern, scalable logistics parks, lease them on long-term agreements, and maintain the facilities ourselves.

A dedicated facility management team supports each site around the clock — handling operations, compliance with local authorities, and infrastructure upkeep so clients can focus on their business.
